# v0.439.0 - One customer form, wherever you start

Adding or editing a customer used to open one of two different forms, depending on where you
started. The Service Desk had a short one whose single Address box held only the street line.
Customers & fleet had a long one that could ask for the same person three times: as the
customer name, as a first and last name, and again as a free-text contact name. Every place
that adds or edits a customer now opens the same form. You choose Household or Business first,
type the person once, and the Register or Save button stays in view. Only the consoles change.

### Where the form opens

- **Service Desk > Book in a repair > New customer...** - for a walk-in, while you book the
  repair in.
- **Service Desk > Customers > Register a customer** - if you typed a search first, the button
  reads Register "what you typed".
- **Service Desk > Customers >** (pick a customer) **> Edit contact details**.
- **Customers & fleet** (in the Server Console, or **Server administration** on a technician
  console): **Add customer...**, **Edit customer...** (in a customer's details) and
  **Rename...** (with a customer selected).

### What the person typing will notice

- **Household or Business comes first.** A household is a person: type the first and last
  name, and the customer is named "First Last" for you. A business has a **Business name**,
  with an optional contact person (first and last name) under it. The Service Desk's customer
  card still calls a household "Residential", and still shows only the street line of the
  address.
- **The person is typed once.** The free-text contact box (the Service Desk's "Contact person",
  Customers & fleet's "Contact name") is gone. A business's contact person is now a first and
  last name.
- **A household's name follows the person.** If you correct a household's first or last name,
  the customer's name on tickets and invoices changes to match - unless the name was set by
  hand under **More details > Shown as**, which then stays as you set it.
- **One list of phone numbers.** Each row is the number, its type (for example Mobile, Home or
  Work) and an extension, and the first number is the main one. **Add another number** adds a
  row; clear a number to remove its row. The Service Desk used to offer a single phone box.
- **Email** is where invoices are sent. The note under it now appears in every copy of the
  form, not only in the Service Desk: the address does not by itself give anyone access to
  this customer's tickets (a customer login is linked to a customer on the Users & groups
  page), and clearing it takes nothing away. Older logins that were never linked still fall
  back to matching this address. When you enter an email that already belongs to another
  customer, the form now asks before saving wherever you opened it (Customers & fleet used to
  save without asking), and it checks the server's current list, so a customer registered a
  minute ago on another PC counts.
- **Address** is the one printed on invoices, in five parts: Street, Apartment or unit, City,
  State / region, and ZIP / postal code. The Service Desk now saves all five. A ZIP / postal
  code made only of digits that is not 5 or 9 digits long gets a reminder of the US format; it
  never stops the save, and a postcode with letters in it is left alone.
- **More details** holds **Referred by** (new in the Service Desk; on Customers & fleet it moved
  here from the main form), a household's **Shown as** name, and anything carried over from an
  older record (see below). It opens by itself when there is something in it you need to see.
- **Register / Save and Cancel stay at the bottom of the window.** They no longer scroll away
  with a long form.

### Two customers with the same name

Two customers still cannot share a name. When the name you typed is taken, the form says so
and now offers a way to tell the two apart, built from the address: a button such as
**Use "John Smith (Oak St)"**, using the street without its house number, or the city when
there is no street. There is no suggestion when neither is filled in. For a household it goes
into **Shown as**, so it stays put if the person's name is corrected later; for a business it
replaces the business name.

### Customers saved by an earlier version

A customer registered or edited before this release may carry a free-text contact name - from
the Service Desk's old Contact person box, or Customers & fleet's Contact name box. Nothing
about it changes unless somebody chooses to change it:

- If that contact says something the name and the person do not, the form shows it under
  **More details** as **Previously recorded contact**, read-only, with two buttons: **Use as
  first/last name** and **Remove**. Use as first/last name splits at the last space, so "Mary
  Ann Smith" becomes Mary Ann / Smith - check it before you save. On a household whose name
  follows the person, it also changes the customer's name. Either choice takes effect when
  you save.
- If you press neither button, or if the old contact only repeats the name or the person, it
  is saved exactly as it was.
- A household with no first and last name on file - most households registered in the Service
  Desk before this release - opens with its name under **More details > Shown as**. Filling in
  the first and last name does not change that name; once they match it, the name follows them
  from the next edit.

### Faster at the counter

- **A search goes into the right box.** Register "what you typed" used to put everything into
  the name. Now a phone number (7 digits or more) goes into Phone, an email address into
  Email, and words into the first and last name.
- **A walk-in is picked for you.** After **New customer...** on the Book in a repair form, the
  new customer is selected in the Customer box, and the device, the fault and the priority you
  had already typed are still there. The form used to be rebuilt, which lost what you had typed
  and left the new customer to be found and picked by hand.
- **New customers start as a Household in the Service Desk** (they used to start as a
  business) and **as a Business on Customers & fleet**. Switch with Household / Business at the
  top of the form.
- **Registering a business from a Service Desk search:** the words land in First and Last name,
  because the desk starts on Household. Choose **Business** and they are copied into Business
  name, but First and Last keep them as the contact person - clear them if they are not a
  person.

### Customers & fleet

- The **Backup quota** section is still in **Edit customer...** and **Rename...**. If your
  account may edit customers but not change quotas, the other details are saved, and a message
  after the form closes says the quota was not changed.

### After you update

Nothing to do. Only the consoles change: the shop server's program is the same apart from its
version number, and nothing changes in the customer console. A console that has not updated
yet keeps its old customer dialogs until it does. The suggestion for a taken name, and the new
customer's name in the Book in a repair picker, need the shop server on v0.438.0 or later. Until
an older server updates itself, a taken name is refused without a suggestion, and a walk-in is
picked under the label "(new customer)".

### Known issues

- **After Edit contact details, the customer card still shows the old details.** The save works, but the card in the Service Desk is not redrawn until you open the customer again from the list. Open it again before you edit it a second time: editing from the old card puts the customer's old name back.
- Still open from v0.438.0: **Stop Safely** can be pushed off the screen in a console window narrower than about 780 pixels; **Choose machine...** on a ticket answers *No such machine.*; and an update can restart the console during a long Data Recovery job (press **Not now (1 hour)** on the countdown).
