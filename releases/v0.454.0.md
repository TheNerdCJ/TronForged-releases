# v0.454.0 - TronForged 0.454.0 - export or erase a customer on request

## TronForged 0.454.0 - export or erase a customer on request

### A customer's data, on request
- **Export customer...** (Server Console > Organizer, on the customer) saves every record the shop holds about that customer as one file: their details, phones, addresses, sites, devices, tickets with their messages and work, invoices and payments, saved cards (brand and last four only), backups and portal accounts. For a customer who asks what you hold about them.
- **Erase customer...** removes it. You type the customer's name to confirm - a mis-click cannot erase anyone. Their tickets' content (messages, work notes, files, parts) and appointment addresses, their devices' records, phones, addresses, saved cards, backups and portal accounts are deleted. **Their invoices and payments are kept** for your tax records, under the name "Erased customer ..." with every contact detail cleared - and so are the forms they signed and the record of who collected each device, which are the shop's signed records and are never changed. Each ticket keeps its number so the invoice still points at it.
- Both need a full-access administrator, and both are recorded in the audit log. **Nothing is ever deleted automatically.**

### PC transfer fix
- **A big profile no longer stops with "the other PC sent a frame of an impossible size".** The list of files the old PC sends first was held to the size of one 1 MB piece; a profile with tens of thousands of files is bigger than that. Update **both** PCs to 0.454.0.

### What to do
- Update the shop server, then the PCs. Export a customer before erasing them if they asked for a copy.
