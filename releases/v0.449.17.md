# v0.449.17 - safer remote desktop

## TronForged 0.449.17 - safer remote desktop

A remote-desktop safety and reliability release.

### Fixed

- **Keys and mouse buttons no longer stay held down on the customer's PC.** If a technician switched to another window, let go of a drag outside the remote picture, or lost the connection while holding a key, that key or button could stay pressed on the customer's PC - even on the sign-in and UAC screens. TronForged now lets go of everything that was held at every step: in the technician's window, on the customer's PC when a technician disconnects, and in the full-control helper.
- **Arrow keys and right Ctrl/Alt behave correctly.** They are now sent the way a real keyboard sends them, so Shift+arrow selects text even with Num Lock on, and AltGr works on European keyboards.
- **Customers can see and end a session that started before they signed in.** If a technician is still working when the customer signs in, TronForged now shows "Remote assistance active" with an **End session** button. Pressing it ends the session at once, and it is recorded.
- **Direct connections are limited to your shop's own network.** A PC now accepts direct remote connections only from its local network when that network is marked Private or Domain. Anywhere else, such as hotel or public Wi-Fi, remote sessions go through your shop server. Existing PCs switch to the narrower rule automatically within about 15 minutes of updating.
- **Ctrl+Alt+Del now works at the lock and sign-in screens.** An earlier setting was wrong, so the button did nothing there while the toolbar said it was sent. The helper fixes the setting the next time it is installed or repaired.
- **A technician signed in on a customer's PC sees only that customer.** The shop server now limits that session to the customer the PC belongs to, plus the repair tools for that PC. Other customers, the shop's queue, invoices, accounting and the fleet stay out of reach, even if the customer takes over the screen.
- **No password "Show" button on a customer's PC,** and the technician session there **locks after 5 minutes** without keyboard or mouse use. Only the same technician can unlock it, and any repair already running keeps going.
- **A customer signing out no longer looks like a crash.** The technician is told the customer signed out and can reconnect to the sign-in screen.
- **Remote control stays on the right screen.** When another user is signed in with "Switch user", or the console runs in a Remote Desktop session, it no longer controls the physical screen of a different user.
- **More reliable remote sessions.** A technician whose laptop went to sleep no longer blocks other technicians from taking control. The optional fast-capture mode no longer leaks graphics memory at each lock screen or UAC prompt. Very wide multi-monitor screens no longer drop the picture on slower PCs.

### What to do

- If your shop network is marked **Public** in Windows, mark it **Private**, or connect through the shop server.
- **Update the shop server with `TronForged.Server-0.449.17.exe`.** The limit that keeps a technician on a customer's PC to that customer is enforced by the server, so it starts only once the server is updated.
- Make sure each customer PC's console has its customer set (it is chosen when the PC is enrolled). A customer PC with no customer set shows no customer records to a technician signed in on it.
