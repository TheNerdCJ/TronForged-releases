# v0.449.21 - TronForged 0.449.21 - Data Migration on a customer's PC, clearer Service desk and Inventory

## TronForged 0.449.21 - Data Migration on a customer's PC, and clearer Service desk and Inventory screens

### Fixed

- **A technician signed in on a customer's PC can use Data Migration.** In 0.449.20 and earlier, signing in with a technician account (an administrator included) on the customer's own PC and opening Data Migration said "Data Migration needs the 'migration' permission on a technician account", even though the account had it. The shop server now allows it there. The customer's own account still cannot use Data Migration.
- **No more white buttons.** A button that was waiting or not yet available showed as a white block in the dark console (for example Save CSV on Stock value). It now dims instead.

### Changed

- **A customer's PC pairs only with the same customer's PCs, or with your shop's own PCs.** Another customer's PC no longer appears in the Data Migration list and cannot be paired. A customer PC not yet assigned to a customer is not held back.
- **Service desk: the customer at the top of a ticket is easier to read.** The customer's name, phone number and device are larger, and when it was booked in sits on its own line. The phone number can be selected and copied.
- **Service desk: Ready by** is now a dark date field in line with the hour and Set. Every date field in the technician console gets the same look.
- **Inventory is reorganised.** Every tab's forms sit on their own card, with a label above each box and short hints where they help. Suppliers, categories and locations list the name first. Stock value's Save CSV appears once there is a report to save.

### What to do

- Update the shop server to this version for the Data Migration fix; then sign out and back in on the customer's PC.
- Consoles update as usual.
