# v0.458.0 - TronForged 0.458.0 - invoices, voids and QuickBooks, done right

## TronForged 0.458.0 - invoices, voids and QuickBooks, done right

### QuickBooks Online export
- A discount or credit line no longer stops the QuickBooks Online invoice export. The export writes every invoice it can, leaves out any invoice with a discount or credit line, and the Server Console lists the ones it left out.
- An invoice that was left out is not marked exported, so it is offered again next time. Book it with the .iif file, the journal entries file, or by hand in QuickBooks.
- An invoice can no longer be sent, or edited, down to a total of zero or less (for example a discount larger than the work).

### Voiding an invoice
- Voiding an invoice frees the time entries that were billed to it, so you can bill that time on the invoice that replaces it. Time cannot be billed onto a void invoice.
- If the invoice was already exported to QuickBooks, the console asks a second time and tells you QuickBooks still holds it. Void it in QuickBooks, or let the next journal entries export book the reversal - not both.

### Dates on your own calendar
- "Overdue" and the aging report now count days on your shop's calendar (the time zone set under the shop's hours), not UTC. An invoice due today no longer shows overdue in the evening.
- An approved estimate's due date is now a day on your calendar.

### Ticket numbers
- Two tickets created at the same moment, from two consoles or a customer's support request, can no longer get the same number.

### Settings that are saved together
- The Square settings and the email settings now save both parts or neither: the access token or password together with the other settings. If a save fails, the page says it was not saved; save again before the server restarts.

### What to do
- Nothing is required. Update the shop server and the consoles as usual.
- If you export to QuickBooks Online, check the export status for invoices it left out and book those another way.
