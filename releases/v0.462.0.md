# v0.462.0 - TronForged 0.462.0 - remote sessions that notice a dropped link, and a tidier helper

## TronForged 0.462.0 - remote sessions that notice a dropped link, and a tidier helper

### Remote Desktop
- **A dropped connection is noticed within about 20 seconds.** Your console and the customer PC now check on each other every 5 seconds. If either side hears nothing for about 20 seconds, it treats the link as dropped and reconnects. Before, a short cable pull or a Wi-Fi drop could leave the picture frozen with no warning.
- **The Remote Desktop window says what is happening.** While it reconnects, its bar says *Reconnecting...* or *Waiting...* with the attempt number, and the picture dims. When the link is back, the picture brightens and you carry on without a second click.
- **Control goes to the right person.** If the technician in control drops off, control returns to that same technician when they reconnect. It never passes to someone else who was only watching.
- **Two customer PCs with the same name stay separate.** Saved devices are now tracked by the PC itself, not by its name. Your existing saved list is carried over.
- **Boxes you filled in survive a reconnect.** Technician, ticket and customer details you applied stay put when the session reconnects.
- **Problems on the customer PC's side are reported.** If the screen capture stops or your input cannot be delivered on the customer PC, that PC now reports it to your shop server, once per problem per session.

### The full-control helper
- **Ctrl+Alt+Delete works right after a reconnect.** It used to say "did not answer" for a second and a half.
- **Install helper works when the helper file is missing.** The button is greyed only when this console has no helper to install.
- **No more false pointers to the log.** If a helper message would send you to `agent-service.log` but the line could not be written there, the message now says so.
- **No leftover screen workers.** If the helper service crashes, its screen worker no longer stays behind and blocks the restarted service.
- **A PC already on the newest release, with an older helper,** is now told to use *Install helper* (or *Enable UAC / lock-screen control* from a remote session). It used to say *Update TronForged*, though there was nothing newer to update to.

### Before-login remote sessions
- **A run needs your own ticket.** A *Run* or a single stage started before Windows sign-in is refused until you push your technician and ticket with *Apply*, the same as on a signed-in PC. The previous technician's ticket is never used for your run.
- **Restarts wait for Windows fixes.** A restart waits while a Windows fix from a remote technician is still running.

### Shop server and consoles
- **Team chat keeps arriving after a reconnect.** A console that reconnected after a network blip now gets team chat messages again without signing in.
- **One online status everywhere.** The machine picker on a ticket now shows the same online status as the machine's fleet tile.
- **Signing out clears the Service Desk.** When a technician signs out on a customer's PC, the Service Desk's tickets, invoices and selections are cleared. A notification clicked after that shows nothing from the shop.
- The background check-in with your shop server keeps one connection open instead of opening a new one every minute.

### What to do
- Update your shop server and every console as usual. The quick drop detection needs both your console and the customer PC on 0.462.0. Until a PC is updated, a drop to it is noticed only when the network gives up, as before.
- Nothing to set up.
