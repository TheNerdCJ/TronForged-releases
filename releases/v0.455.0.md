# v0.455.0 - TronForged 0.455.0 - people on an account, customer sign-ins by email, PC Transfer and update fixes

## TronForged 0.455.0 - people on an account, and customer sign-ins by email

### People on a customer's account
- The customer form has a new section, **People on this account**. Add the people a customer allows to act for them - a name, phone and email - and tick what each may do: **May make decisions on the account**, **May pick up devices**, **Send them updates**.
- "Send them updates" emails that person a copy of each invoice (even when the customer has no email of their own), and ticket updates when you have switched those on.
- The Service Desk search finds a customer by any of their people, and the customer card lists them.

### Pickup
- **Picked up...** now lists everyone allowed to pick up. Choose who came, or type a name under *Someone else* - the record says they were not on the list. Tick that you checked their photo ID; the ID itself is never recorded.
- The customer card shows a **Ready to pick up** list of their finished repairs.
- **Picked up...** is also on that list and on the invoice view once the repair is finished. If a pickup was recorded wrongly, record it again from the same button: the new record corrects the old one, which is kept.

### Sign-ins sent by email
- When your shop's email is set up, a new customer saved with an email - and a person added with one - is emailed their own sign-in. For anyone already on file, press **Send login details** in the customer form. Updating never emails your existing customers.
- The username is their email. The password works once; they choose their own the first time they sign in, and they can never keep a password you sent.
- A password you type for a customer on Users & groups works the same way: they choose their own the first time they sign in.
- **Customer passwords** (Server Console > Settings > Security) stops a customer reusing a password from the last 365 days (you can change the days, or set 0 to turn it off).
- A login made at the counter by someone who does not manage backups cannot see that customer's backups until someone who does presses **Allow their backups...**. A person's own sign-in never sees backups.
- If the customer's email changes, **Replace their login** deletes the old sign-in and sends new details to the new address.

### Ticket updates by email (off until you switch them on)
- On the **Email** page, tick **Email customers when the shop replies to or finishes a ticket** to email your replies and a ticket's resolution to the customer and to the people ticked for updates. What customers write is never emailed.
- **Update every console first**, and set **Reply-To** to a mailbox someone reads - customers will reply.
- A customer can have their own **Ticket updates go to** address, or **No ticket update emails**; invoices still go to the billing email.
- At most one email per customer every 15 minutes; an update not sent within 12 hours is dropped and the ticket says so.

### PC Transfer
- **The files go to the right user.** On the new PC the transfer now goes into the old PC's user (found by name, or created) unless you choose another user - before, a transfer started without touching the list went to whoever was signed in. A transfer that resumes after a restart goes to the same user.
- **Taskbar pins move** - the pinned programs and their order appear at the user's next sign-in.
- **The screen stays awake** for the whole transfer (and for repair runs, disk images and downloads).
- **The text during a transfer is readable** - several lines were drawn in black on the dark page.
- Each transfer now reports how it went (finished or where it stopped, how much moved, how fast, Wi-Fi or not) to your shop server's fleet analyzer - counts and sizes only, never a file, program or user name.

### Updates
- **Install and restart** is on the customer console's Updates card too, once an update is downloaded and checked.
- An update checks its signatures and the free space on the drive BEFORE it downloads, so a nearly full disk is never filled and an unsigned release is never downloaded; a dropped piece of a download is retried.
- **Update from a USB drive** now works on an installed PC, and picks the newest version on the stick.
- A rolled-back update no longer leaves the background service restarting every minute, and the rollback is reported to your shop server; a recalled release is not pulled back in by the automatic pull.

### Also
- **New customer...** is now in the Server Console's **Assign device** as well as the approvals window: register a customer there and they are picked for the PC straight away.

### Before you update
- This release changes the database. To go back to an older version you need a database copy from BEFORE the update, restored by the older server program - see "Going back from v0.455.0" in the server setup guide.
