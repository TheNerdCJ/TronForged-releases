# TronForged v0.449.14 - release notes

### Printing

- Every printed page and report - service reports, device and health reports, invoices, signed forms and erase certificates - is laid out for US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) paper.
- The printed content is kept 7.26 in wide, so the same pages also print on A4 paper without anything cut off at the edge or shrunk to fit.
- Wide tables wrap long paths and names inside the page, column headings repeat on every page, and a row or heading is never split across two pages.

### Secure Data Erasure

- A drive in a USB enclosure is now listed by the drive's own model and serial number, read from the drive through
  the enclosure. Many enclosures report a made-up serial (such as 0123456789000000000), so two drives in two
  enclosures could look almost the same. The phrase you type, the check just before writing and the certificate all
  use the drive's own serial.
- When the enclosure does not pass the drive's own details on (and for NVMe drives in USB enclosures), the page says
  the serial shown is the enclosure's, not the drive's. The drive is then matched by the enclosure's serial and its
  exact size, and the certificate says the same.
