# v0.462.1 - TronForged 0.462.1 - Temporary Fleet: one-time remote support for any Windows PC

## TronForged 0.462.1 - Temporary Fleet: one-time remote support for any Windows PC

### What is new
- **Help a customer whose PC is not enrolled with your shop.** The Technician console has a new **Temporary Fleet** page in the Customers group. Press **Start a session** and you get a six-digit PIN and one sentence to read to the customer: go to your shop's web address and enter the PIN.
- **The customer downloads a small one-time support program** (about 24 MB). After a right PIN, the page offers one download for a Windows PC. Windows asks the customer for administrator approval when it opens, so your technician can work with programs that run as administrator and, only if the customer allows it, install the full TronForged console. The support program itself installs nothing: no service, no startup entry, no saved sign-in and no stored consent. Closing it, or restarting the PC, ends the session.
- **The customer stays in charge.** The program names the technician who wants to connect, and nothing happens until the customer clicks **Allow**. A banner shows while you are connected.
- **You connect through the Remote Desktop window you already use.** Only the technician who started a session can connect to it. The technician or an administrator can end it. A temporary session never shows the unattended-access controls, and a reconnect within 2 minutes resumes without asking the customer again.
- **Install TronForged on the customer's PC.** While you are connected, the Temporary Fleet row offers **Install TronForged**. The customer is asked first; the download is checked the same way as a console update before the normal installer starts, with its usual install choices and licence. Your shop server's audit log records the version installed, its SHA-256 and the address it went to.

### Built to resist misuse
- A PIN lasts 10 minutes and works once.
- Wrong PINs are limited: 5 from one address in 10 minutes lock that address for 15 minutes, and 30 from anywhere pause every PIN check for 10 minutes.
- One technician can have 5 open temporary sessions, and the shop 50.
- Every PIN check, download, connect and end is written to your shop server's audit log. The PIN itself is never recorded.
- The page shows only the PIN form until a right PIN is entered. It lists nothing about your shop or your customers.
- Your enrolled PCs (the Unattended Fleet) are not changed in any way.

### Good to know
- **Temporary Fleet is off until an administrator turns it on**, with the switch at the top of the Temporary Fleet page. Turning it off ends every open temporary session.
- **Restarting the shop server ends every temporary session.** The customer then needs a new PIN and a new download.
- **The support program is not code-signed yet**, so Windows warns the customer before it runs. The browser may say the file is not commonly downloaded, SmartScreen may show *Windows protected your PC* (choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**), and the administrator prompt names an unknown publisher. Tell the customer to expect this.
- **Windows only.** On any other system the page says that your remote support needs a Windows PC. Windows 11 on ARM runs the program; Windows 10 on ARM does not.
- This feature has not yet been tried on real customer hardware. Please report anything that does not work as described.

### What to do
- Update your shop server and your Technician consoles as usual.
- Your shop server needs its web transport (WSS) turned on, and its web address needs a certificate that web browsers trust: a Cloudflare-proxied (orange-cloud) name, or a certificate from a public certificate authority on the server. A Cloudflare Origin CA certificate on a name that is not proxied does not work for this feature.
- The support program is built into your shop server. **Install TronForged** hands out the newest console your server offers its own consoles, so keep your release store current.
- Then have an administrator turn on Temporary Fleet on the Temporary Fleet page. The setup guide (SERVER_SETUP, section G.6) has the details.
