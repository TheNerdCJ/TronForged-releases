# v0.449.4 - TronForged 0.449.4 - a new look for all three consoles, booking jobs in, the schedule, reminders, signed forms and parts

This release follows v0.449.2, and these notes cover everything that changed since then. Version 0.449.3 was prepared but never released: its final review found problems, they are fixed here, and everything it brought arrives with this release. So this release brings two things: the new look for all three consoles - the technician console, the customer console and the Server Console - with a choice of where every backup goes (the 0.449.3 work), and booking jobs in, the schedule, appointment reminders, signed intake and pickup forms, and parts on a ticket (new in 0.449.4). The shop server's program changes too, so update the shop server first. The licence changes; the changes are listed at the end.

## Part 1 - The new look (prepared as 0.449.3)

### The technician console

- **The pages sit in a rail down the left side**, grouped as Bench (Automated service, Triage, Manual stages, Tools, Windows fixes), Customers (Service desk, Schedule, Fleet and remote, Team chat), Data (Backup and restore, Disk images, Data recovery, Forensics) and Install and tune (ISO files, Reinstall, Power, Network optimizer, Benchmarks and QC, Maintenance), with Reports and Settings at the bottom. You see only the pages your account may open, and a group with none of them shows no heading.
- **Six pages have new names**: Manual Tools is now **Tools**, Remote is **Fleet and remote**, Chat is **Team chat**, Backup/Restore is **Backup and restore**, Imaging is **Disk images**, and Benchmarks & QC is **Benchmarks and QC**. Each does what it did before, for the same people.
- **The window's smallest width is now 860 pixels**, to make room for the rail.
- **Automated service shows the run as a timeline of its stages, and how long is left.** The estimate comes only from your own shop's runs of the last 90 days, once a stage has run three times. Until every stage still to run has three, it says "no estimate yet" - it never adds up only the stages it happens to know.
- **The Service desk opens in lanes**: Checked in, On the bench, Waiting and Ready for pickup, with the list one click away (each technician's choice is remembered). A waiting ticket can say what it waits for - Parts, Customer reply or Other - with a short note such as "ETA Fri". The number beside Service desk in the rail counts the open tickets (checked in, on the bench, waiting) among the newest 200 the desk has loaded. Tickets still change status in the ticket itself, not by dragging.
- **Fleet and remote opens on a list of which PCs you can get into right now**: how many PCs, how many are online and how many need attention; a search box; and for each PC its remote readiness, version and when it was last seen. Choose one to see every check - unattended access, the sign-in screen, the full-control helper, whether a repair is running on it, whether its display is on or asleep, its screen size, how long its last check-in took to reach the shop server, and its version - with a sentence saying what to do, and a Remote in button. A line is left out when nobody has said: a PC or shop server still on an older version, or the PC has not checked in for 30 minutes (the Sign-in screen, System helper, Repair run, Display, Screen size, Connection and Version lines; Unattended access is always shown). The fleet tiles use the same verdict, so their light now turns amber when a PC is not ready for remote control.
- **The diagnostics report's antivirus line says what it found**: protected, real-time protection off, definitions out of date, or could not tell. A PC protected by another antivirus product is no longer reported as having real-time protection disabled.

### The customer console

- **Customers no longer run safe fixes from their own console.** Your technicians run the same safe fixes on the PC for them - remotely from Fleet and remote, or signed in at the PC.
- **The customer console now shows Home, Messages, Backups, Reports and Settings.** Home greets the customer by the name you filed the PC under, shows your phone number and today's opening hours, and has an "Ask the shop for help" box - what they type arrives on your Service desk exactly as a request from the tray does. It also shows the newest message you sent, and the PC's storage, app version, connection, antivirus and last backup at the shop. Messages is the same conversation as the tray's "Messages from your shop" window. Backups lists the PC's backups at the shop.
- **Opening hours appear only after you set them** (see "After you update"). Until then the customer sees no hours at all.
- **What a customer reads about a waiting ticket never includes your own reason or note.** The waiting reason and note are for your staff only.

### The Server Console

- **The pages are grouped**: Status and Get started; People (Users & groups, Customers & fleet, Service desk, Windows updates); Money (Accounting, Square); Data (Backups, Audit log); This server (Email, Branding, Set up server); and Settings last.
- **Status opens with "Needs your attention"**: every amber or red line on the page, with the button that fixes it where there is one, and a count beside Status.
- **A "Console versions" card** shows which versions your PCs ran over the last 7 days, how many are two or more releases behind (with a button to see them), and how many older consoles did not say.
- **Branding now holds your shop's opening hours** - time zone, each day's hours and closed dates. Customers see them on Home and emailed invoices print them. Once they are set, the Email page shows the invoice's hours line made from them; the text typed there is used only until then.

### Where backups go

- **Every backup and every disk image now asks where it goes: Shop server, External drive, or Both.** Both writes the copy to your shop drive, checks it, sends that same copy to the shop server, and is finished only when both copies are confirmed. If the upload fails, the checked copy on the drive is kept, the page says which copy failed, and Retry sends it again, continuing from where it stopped when it can (within 72 hours). A job whose upload was cut off - even by closing the console - is offered Retry the next time.
- **A shop drive is a drive you have confirmed.** An external copy goes only on a drive you confirmed will hold customer data - never the Windows drive or a drive being captured, and a customer's plugged-in drive is never offered unless you confirm it. "Add a shop drive..." adds one. Nothing is carried over from before: after this update, on each technician PC, the first External or Both use asks once. A drive keeps its status when its letter changes; another drive that takes the old letter does not. The automatic backup after a service run refuses a different drive on the letter it was set up with. The system-image drive lists follow the same rule.
- **A disk image sent only to the shop server keeps the copy it was made from on this PC**, and the page says where; delete it from the image library when you no longer need it. Nothing deletes a copy by itself.
- **A Windows system image still goes to an external drive only.** For a copy on the shop server, capture a disk image instead.
- **The shop server now confirms each copy it stores.** It compares its own fingerprint of what arrived with the one the console sent; a backup is not marked done until it matches. An interrupted upload of a large disk image now resumes instead of starting over. If the fingerprints do not match, the next try starts afresh, and a file that changed in the meantime is read again.

## Part 2 - Booking jobs in, the schedule, reminders and signed forms (new in 0.449.4)

### Booking a job in

- **"Book in a job" is the one way to open a ticket at the counter.** Choose the job type, the customer and who dropped it off, write what is wrong in the customer's words, choose the technicians, and say which device it is. For a remote support job or a service call, book the appointment in the same step - for a visit, at the customer's address, one of their sites, or another address. A service call may have no device at all.
- **Items brought in with the device** - charger, bag, cables, mouse, external drive, USB stick, case, or anything you type, each with its condition - are listed on the ticket and on the intake form, and checked off at pickup.
- **Job types are your shop's own.** Your shop server starts with In-house repair, Warranty repair, Remote support, Service call and Other; add, rename and retire them on the Server Console's new Service desk page. A type is never deleted, and tickets keep showing a retired type.
- **Several technicians on one job.** Every ticket has a primary technician and anyone else assigned; "Add me" puts you on a ticket, and the ticket shows that you did. Every message in a ticket names the person who wrote it, and the work list says who ran each job.

### The device's sign-in password

- **A password the customer gives for their device is kept sealed on your shop server**, with a key that the database files, its daily snapshots and backup files do not contain. Only a technician assigned to that ticket can show it - anyone else, an owner included, presses "Add me" first. It shows for 30 seconds with no copy button, and every look is recorded on the ticket and in the audit log. It never appears in messages, reports, emails, exports, AI features, what the customer sees, or the logs. It stays with the ticket until someone at your shop removes it.

### Appointments and the Schedule

- **A new Schedule page** shows your shop's week in your shop's time zone, with each visit under its technicians; choose a visit to see its details and every reminder, move it, cancel it, or open its ticket. Moving a visit does not email the customer - call them; they get the new reminder times.
- **Appointment reminders** go out at two times you choose on the Server Console's Settings page - by default 24 hours and 1 hour before; either can be switched off. Each technician on the visit gets a notice on their technician console. Unless you untick "Remind the customer", the customer gets an email from your shop's mailbox and, when the ticket is linked to their PC running the customer console, a notice on that PC, with the time in that PC's own time zone.
- **Every reminder says what really happened**: scheduled, sent (your mail server took it), shown (a console received it), or not sent with the reason - for example that the customer has no email address, that your shop's email is not set up, or that the visit was booked after the reminder's time. A reminder whose time had already passed when the visit was booked is not sent late, and nothing claims the customer read it.

### Signed intake and pickup forms

- **The customer signs an intake form when a device is booked in, and a pickup form when it is collected** - on the screen with a mouse, a finger or a pen, on a Topaz signature pad, or on paper. Each signed form is kept exactly as signed, with its date, version and the staff member who took it, and your shop server makes a PDF copy and attaches it to the ticket, where the customer's account can see it too. You can print it, save it, or email it to the customer as one PDF attachment. The PDF copy stays with the signed form: it cannot be removed from the ticket.
- **The form texts that come with TronForged are starting points only, not legal advice.** Every copy says "STARTER TEXT - this shop has not reviewed it" until you save your own version on the Server Console's Service desk page. Have your own lawyer review them before you rely on them.
- **Topaz signature pads** work through Topaz's own SigWeb service, which you install from Topaz Systems on the PC with the pad. TronForged does not include or download any Topaz software, and offers "Sign on the pad" only when that service answers on the same PC.

### Pickup

- **"Picked up..." records who collected a finished job** - the account holder or someone else by name - with a reminder to check their photo ID, and each item brought in marked returned or still at the shop. Closing a ready-for-pickup ticket asks for it, with "Close without recording" for the cases that need it.

### Parts a ticket waits for

- **Record each part a ticket waits for**: its name, supplier, order or tracking number, the day it was ordered and when it is expected. Adding a part puts the ticket in Waiting; when the last open part is received or voided the ticket goes back on the bench, and the ticket records who did it. A reply from the customer does not take a ticket off a parts wait while a part is still open; you still hear about the reply. The waiting card shows the expected day.

### What your customer reads about a waiting ticket

- **Waiting on a part:** "Waiting for a part - expected Fri 16 Oct" (or "Waiting for a part" with no date yet) - never which part or from whom.
- **Waiting on the customer (or when no reason was chosen):** "<your shop's name> is waiting on a reply from you. Open ticket #1042 to reply."
- **Any other wait:** "<your shop's name> will update you on this ticket."

### The Server Console

- **A new Service desk page** (under People) holds your job types and the intake and pickup forms, with a live preview of each form and every earlier version kept. The reminder times are on the Settings page.
- **The backup location's free space is now measured by the shop server**, so a network share (NAS) shows its real free space on the Status and Backups pages instead of "free space unknown". When the share cannot be reached, or refuses the server's sign-in, the line says so and appears under "Needs your attention"; the shop server's refusal of new uploads when the share is nearly full now works on a share too.

## Before you update

- **Update the shop server first** (Set up server > Update server, on the server PC), then the consoles. On its first start the shop server adds its new tables to its database; nothing already there is changed.
- **Until the shop server is updated, a console on this release cannot send a backup or disk image to it**: Shop server and Both are switched off with "this shop server is older than v0.449.3 and cannot confirm the copy it stores - update the server", and the automatic backup after a service run is stored but marked not confirmed. External drive still works.
- A console on this release that talks to an older server shows the new pages, but what only the new server keeps is left out or says so - never a guess. In particular: "Book in a job" records only the customer, what is wrong, the priority and the device, and says "This shop server is older than this console - update the server to record items, passwords, technicians and appointments." (the password box is switched off, and nothing typed there is sent anywhere); a ticket says the server does not keep job types, technicians, items or appointments; the Schedule says the server does not keep appointments; signing a form says the server cannot keep signed forms; a waiting ticket cannot say what it waits for ("This shop server does not keep a waiting reason - update the server."), so there are no parts either; closing a finished ticket works as before, with no pickup; and the Server Console's Service desk page and reminder times say the server does not keep them. The new look's server facts are missing too: the Automated service page says "no estimate yet", Fleet and remote shows no Display, Screen size, Repair run or Connection lines, the customer's Home shows no last backup, opening hours cannot be set, and the Console versions card says the server is too old to count versions.
- **A backup upload in progress while the server updates starts again from the beginning once.** Let large uploads finish before updating, if you can.

## After you update

- **Set your opening hours** on the Server Console's Branding page. Until you do, customers see no hours, invoices keep printing the Email page's text, and the Schedule shows 8:00 am to 6:00 pm.
- **Review the job types and the forms** on the Server Console's Service desk page, and have your lawyer review the intake and pickup texts before you rely on them.
- **Check the reminder times** on the Settings page, and that your shop's email is set up on the Email page - without it, customer reminders are not sent.
- **Customer reminders are on by default.** Every Remote support or Service call visit you book has "Remind the customer" ticked, so once your shop's email is set up, the customer gets reminder emails from the first visit you book. Untick it on a visit, or switch a reminder time off on the Server Console's Settings page, if you are not ready. Nothing is sent for anything booked before this update.
- **Confirm your shop drives again**: on each technician PC, the first External or Both backup asks once which drive is a shop drive.
- **If your shop's brand colour was never changed**, every console keeps the blue it has today. To use the new orange look, set Branding's accent colour to #F28C28.
- **Update your technician consoles and customer PCs.** Technicians get appointment notices only on a technician console running this release, and a customer PC shows its readiness facts (display, screen size, repair in progress, check-in time) and appointment notices only when it runs this release.

## The licence

The licence changes in these places:

- **Section 17.2** now says that your shop's technicians, not the customer, run safe fixes on the customer's PC.
- **Section 12.2** adds the four facts a PC now reports on its check-in (display on or asleep, main screen size, whether a repair is running, and how long its last check-in took), which the shop server holds in memory and does not store, and says that the shop server stores the version each console last reported and when.
- **Section 12.3** says that a device sign-in password recorded with a ticket is sealed with a key the database files, snapshots and backup files do not contain, and **section 12.5** that it stays until someone at the shop removes it or the ticket is deleted.
- **Section 16.3** adds appointment reminders and copies of signed forms to the email the shop server sends, and says what each carries.
- **New section 16.6** (for the shop) and **new section 19.4** (for the customer) describe booking in, device passwords, signed forms and reminders. Section 16.6 also says what is recorded at collection (who collected the device, whether their photo ID was checked, and which items were returned), and 19.4 that a form the customer signs is kept exactly as signed, can be seen in their account with the shop, and can be emailed to them.
- **Schedule A** names the three typefaces the consoles now use (Barlow, Barlow Semi Condensed and JetBrains Mono, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1), whose licence texts are installed with the software, and a new **Part 4** names Topaz Systems SigWeb as software the shop installs itself and TronForged does not distribute.

## Not yet tested on hardware

These changes were tested on the build machine only. None has yet been run on a real PC: the new layout at different display scalings on a small laptop screen, the customer's "Ask the shop for help" reaching your Service desk, a PC's display going to sleep and waking, the check-in time on a real network, Both to a real USB drive and shop server with the network pulled mid-upload, the Server Console's attention list and version card on a real shop server, booking jobs in on a real shop server, reminders arriving on real consoles, customer PCs and mailboxes, signing with a real mouse, touchscreen, pen and Topaz pad, printing a form on a real printer, parts, and a real NAS's free space.
