## v0.431.0 - Adding a new PC is more forgiving, and says what went wrong

### A dropped connection no longer stops a new PC from joining

When a brand-new PC asks to join your shop, it tries your address and then the web ports
your address can be reached on. Until now each of those ports got exactly one try. If the
first try on the port your shop actually uses was lost - a busy Wi-Fi link, or security
software on the PC holding the connection up - the PC gave up and told the customer the
port "did not get through", even though your server was working.

Now, when one of those ports gets no answer but another port on the same address does, the
PC tries the silent port a second time before it gives up. If nothing answers anywhere, it
gives up exactly as quickly as before.

### When a PC still cannot reach you, the message says what happened on each port

The window used to name only the port that got furthest. It now ends with one line that
lists every port it tried and what happened on it, for example:

> What each web port did - 8443: took the connection, then TLS never finished (tried 2
> times); 2096, 2087, 2083, 2053, 443: completed TLS, but nothing behind it answered as the
> shop server.

If a customer reads you that line, or sends you a photo of it, you can tell whether the
problem is on their PC, their network, or your server.

### A PC on your own bench: use the server's local address

When a public address does not work, the window now suggests your server's local address
in case the PC is in the shop. Before, it only suggested this when nothing answered at all.

A PC on your bench can always join over your server's local address (shown on the Server
Console's *Customers & fleet* page), because that route never goes out to the internet.
Once it has joined, it learns your public address by itself and still connects from the
customer's home. The setup guide and README now say this in the step where you add a
customer PC.

### Updating

Only the consoles change. The shop server's program is the same apart from its version
number.
