# v0.449.2 - TronForged 0.449.2 - remote screen after a restart or dropped connection, and update fixes

This release follows v0.449.1, and these notes cover everything that changed since then. The changes are all in the consoles: the shop server's program works exactly as before, your shop's database is unchanged, and the licence is unchanged. The server program is still published with this version number, so your shop server takes it like any other release.

This release fixes remote access to customer PCs, and two ways a PC could stop taking updates.

### What was wrong

- **Remote control could show a black screen, or one picture and then nothing.** Two things caused it. When the connection to a customer PC dropped for a moment, the console reconnected by itself but never asked the PC for its screen again, so the window kept showing the last picture while the Remote tab said *Connected*. And if the customer PC had not yet noticed that the old connection was gone, it let you back in read-only and kept sending the picture to the dead connection.
- **The full-control helper was restarted at every boot and every sign-in.** The helper is the part of TronForged that lets a technician see and control the sign-in screen and Windows' security prompts. The before-login service (which starts with Windows) and the console (which starts when someone signs in) each carried a slightly different copy of the same helper version, so at every boot and every sign-in one of them stopped the helper, replaced it and started it again. A technician connecting while that happened was told the helper was not installed.
- **The before-login service could miss Windows' start-up window.** It did its start-up work before telling Windows it had started. On a slow PC Windows could give up waiting and mark the service as failed.

### What changed

- **The helper starts with the computer and stays up.** Every part of TronForged now carries the same helper file, and a helper of the same version is never replaced. A helper that was just installed or failed to restart is started again straight away.
- **Windows restarts the helper if it ever stops** - after 5 seconds, then 15 seconds, then 60 seconds. New installs get this when the helper is installed. Enrolled customer PCs that already have the helper get it by themselves within about 15 minutes of taking this update. On a technician's own PC that has the helper, click **Install helper** on the Remote tab once after updating to add it.
- **The before-login service tells Windows it has started first**, then does its start-up work, and checks the helper before anything else.
- **At the sign-in screen, a remote screen asked for while the helper is still starting waits for it** (up to 30 seconds) and says so, instead of saying the helper is not installed. If it still does not answer, the message says whether the helper is not running or not answering. "Not installed" now means exactly that.
- **The helper keeps the customer PC's display awake while a technician is watching.** If the screen at the sign-in screen really is dark, you are told "the screen on this PC is off or black" instead of being shown a black picture with no explanation.
- **Signing in at the customer PC no longer cuts off a technician** who is connected through the before-login service. The service keeps the session (for up to 30 minutes) and the console takes over when it ends.
- **A dropped remote session picks its picture back up.** Every reconnect asks the customer PC for its screen again, and the Remote Desktop window's bar says *Reconnected*.
- **You keep control after a reconnect.** If the customer PC still holds your old connection, it hands control back to your same session. Another technician's session is never handed control this way.
- **The Remote Desktop window says why the picture is black.** Messages from the customer PC - the helper is not installed or not answering, its screen capture stopped, you are read-only - now appear on the window's own bar, not only on the Remote tab behind it.
- **The F12 overlay counts pictures that arrived but could not be shown.**

### The update fixes

- **A PC whose update download was interrupted could stop updating.** If a download was cut off and a newer release came out before it finished, the PC joined the two downloads together, rejected the result, and kept failing on every try after that. A partial download now belongs to one release, a different release starts clean, and a download that fails its check is thrown away.
- **A refused update made the before-login service restart every minute.** When the service could not swap in a new version, it still recorded the update as applied, then restarted itself every minute waiting for a version that never arrived. It now records an update only when the swap really happened, and ignores a record left behind by an earlier version.

Both stop happening once this release is running on the PC. A PC that is already stuck may not be able to download this release by itself - see "After you update".

### Before you update

Nothing is needed on the shop server. If your Server Console updates the server automatically, it does so as usual - only when the shop is idle, with the server offline for about a minute while it restarts.

### After you update

- **A PC already stuck restarting its before-login service every minute** cannot fix itself with that service: let the console on that PC take this update (sign in at the PC and let it update), or install this release on it by hand.
- **A PC that stayed on an older version while the rest of your PCs moved on** may have the interrupted-download problem - its update status keeps saying the download failed its integrity check. Its old updater cannot get past this on its own: install this release on it by hand.
- **To check that a customer PC's helper restarts itself**, open a Command Prompt as administrator on that PC about 15 minutes after it updates and type `sc qfailure TronForgedHelper`. It should list three RESTART actions.
- **Update your technician consoles too.** Picking the picture back up after a dropped connection is done by the technician's console, so it needs this release.
- **A customer PC that has not yet taken this update** cannot hand control back after a dropped connection: a reconnect may come back read-only until that PC notices the old connection is gone. Close the Remote Desktop window and connect again after a minute.

### Not yet tested on hardware

These changes were tested on the build machine only. None has yet been run on a real customer PC: the helper starting with Windows and restarting itself, the display staying awake at the sign-in screen, a sign-in during a before-login session, a dropped session picking up its picture (on your network or across the internet), and the update download and restart fixes.
