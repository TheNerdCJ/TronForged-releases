## v0.386.0 - the Recycle Bin result, and customer names on the report

Two places where the same PC could produce two different answers depending on which version
of PowerShell happened to be installed on it.

- **Emptying the Recycle Bin now reports what the bin actually did.** It used to decide from
  an error code, and on a PC with a second drive that has no recycle bin - a USB stick, a
  backup disk, a mapped drive - it reported "Failed" on one engine and "Emptied" on another,
  for the same bin. It now counts the bin before and after and tells you the result, including
  "Already empty" and "only some items were removed".
- **Accented names survive an interrupted run.** If a repair was interrupted and resumed
  later, the technician's name and the customer's name could come back mangled on the printed
  report, along with your own business name. Fixed.

Assets: `TronForged-0.386.0.exe` + `.sha256`, `TronForged.Server-0.386.0.exe` + `.sha256`.
