# v0.443.0 - For the record only - tray alerts when a customer asks for help (install v0.446.0)

Published for the record after v0.446.0. Install the latest release instead: it includes everything here and later fixes.

**This release is published for the record only.** It is not offered as an update: shop servers, consoles and new installs never receive it. Install v0.446.0 or later instead - it carries everything below plus fixes to it, and its release notes cover v0.443.0 through v0.446.0.

What v0.443.0 added:

- **A technician hears when a customer asks for help.** A customer's request used to arrive silently: the only sign on a technician console was a new row in the Service Desk list, which stops refreshing while the console is minimized. Now every technician console signed in with an account that can manage tickets asks the shop server once a minute and shows a Windows notification when a customer opens a ticket - with **Request support from a technician** in their PC's tray, or through their shop account - or answers on one. Several at once become one alert, and clicking it opens that ticket in the Service Desk.
- Tickets booked at the counter, staff replies and internal notes are not announced, and neither is anything already waiting when the console signed in.
- The alerts need the technician console and the shop server both on v0.443.0 or later.

Do not install this version: while a technician is signed in on a customer's PC with **Technician sign-in...**, that PC shows the alerts too, naming other customers and their tickets. That is fixed in v0.445.0, and v0.446.0 carries the fix.
