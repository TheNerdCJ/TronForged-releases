# v0.441.0 - Address suggestions as you type - off until you switch them on

Customer addresses no longer have to be typed letter by letter. Your shop server can now suggest matching addresses as the street is typed, and for a shop in the United States it can check whether a house number falls within an address range the U.S. Census Bureau knows. Both are optional and **off until an administrator switches them on**. While they are off, nothing changes and no address leaves your server. When they are on, your shop server - never a console - sends the address text to the outside services you chose, and nothing else about the customer: no name, email address or phone number. This release changes the consoles and the shop server's program. It does not change the structure of your shop's database, and nothing changes in the customer console.

**We recommend leaving address lookup switched off for now.** It works, but typing in the customer form with suggestions showing has problems (see **Known issues**) that an upcoming update fixes. While it is off, nothing in this release affects you.

### Switching it on

- In the Server Console, open **Customers & fleet** and click **Address lookup...**. On a technician console with permission to administer the server, the same button is under **Server administration** > **Customers & fleet**. Seeing and changing these settings needs an administrator account.
- Enter **the shop's country** as two letters, such as US. It is required, and suggestions cover that country only.
- Tick **Switch address lookup on**, then tick the services you want:
  - **Suggest addresses as the street is typed (OpenStreetMap data, through Photon)**.
  - **Check a chosen United States address with the U.S. Census Bureau**. This is for a shop in the US only; for any other country the window refuses to save it switched on.
- Optional: **Photon server**, to use a Photon server of your own instead of komoot's (it must be an https:// address); **the shop's location** as a latitude and a longitude, to rank nearby suggestions first; and a **Contact email for these services**. Leave the **Census geocoder** box as it is.
- Your shop server must be able to reach these services over HTTPS: photon.komoot.io (or your own Photon server) and geocoding.geo.census.gov.
- Click **Save**. The settings are kept by the shop server and survive a restart. If the server cannot write them to disk, the window says so: they work until the server restarts, and are then lost. Changes to these settings are recorded in the audit log.
- When it opens, the window shows how many lookups have left the server since it last started, whether a service is paused, and the last problem it had.
- A customer form that is already open does not pick up the change. Close it and open it again.

### What is sent, and to whom

- **Suggestions** go to the Photon service run by komoot, or to the Photon server you name. Each request carries what is typed in the **Street** box (from four characters on, after a short pause in typing), the shop's country, and the shop's location if you entered one. The City, State, ZIP and Apartment or unit boxes are not sent for suggestions.
- **Checks** go to the U.S. Census Bureau's geocoder. Each request carries the street, city, state and ZIP of the address being checked - never the apartment or unit.
- Every request also names TronForged and its version, and carries the contact email only if you typed one in these settings. It is never filled in from your shop's own details. Each service also sees your shop server's public IP address and when each request was made.
- Consoles never contact these services themselves. Everything goes through the shop server.
- Answers are kept in the shop server's memory, so the same street typed again is not sent again. They are gone when the server restarts. The audit log records each lookup that left the server - who asked, which service, how many characters and results, how long it took and how it ended - never the address itself.
- Suggestions are OpenStreetMap data. The credit "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors" appears as a link under every list of suggestions, because OpenStreetMap's licence requires it.

### In the customer form

Suggestions and checks appear in the customer form in the Service Desk and on Customers & fleet. They work in the main address and, when mail or packages go somewhere else, in those addresses too. They are offered to staff accounts that can register customers.

- After at least four characters in **Street** and a short pause, up to five matching addresses appear under the box, with the first one highlighted. A line under the Street box says what is sent.
- **Enter** takes the highlighted suggestion. It does not register or save the customer. Click any suggestion to take that one instead. **Escape**, while the cursor is in the Street box, closes the list and leaves the form open.
- Taking a suggestion replaces what is in **Street**, **City**, **State / region** and **ZIP / postal code** with the suggestion's. **Apartment or unit** is never touched. If the suggestion has no ZIP, the ZIP box is cleared - even if you had typed one - so check it before you save.
- Nothing is saved until you click **Register** or **Save**, as before.
- When a lookup cannot be made, the line under the Street box says why - for example, your hourly allowance is used up, or the service asked your server to slow down. Type the address as usual. A lookup never stops you saving.

### Checking a United States address

- With the check switched on, taking a suggestion checks it straight away, and a **Check this address** button under the City / State / ZIP row checks an address typed by hand. A check needs the street, and either the ZIP or the city and state.
- The answer is one of three:
  - "The house number is within a known address range (U.S. Census Bureau)."
  - The Census Bureau has no range containing this house number. That is common for new builds, PO boxes and rural routes, and the address may still be right.
  - The check is not available right now. The address can still be saved as typed.
- When a check cannot be made at all - for example, the hourly allowance is used up - the form says why instead.
- **This is not a postal check.** It does not confirm that mail can be delivered to the address, and TronForged never calls an address "verified".
- The Census Bureau usually writes an address in capitals, with abbreviations (for example 12 OAK ST). When its version differs from what is in the form, a **Use this format** button offers it, and a ZIP that differs from yours is pointed out. Nothing in the form changes unless you click the button.
- When a check finds the house number in a known range, saving the customer keeps a note with the record that the address was checked, and when - also when you keep your own version of the address rather than the Census Bureau's. The note is not shown on screen in this release. If the address is changed later, the note is dropped the next time the customer is saved.

### Limits that keep the free services usable

- The shop server paces its requests for the whole shop: about two suggestion requests a second, and one check a second. When it is busy, the form says so - keep typing, or type the address.
- Each account can make up to 120 suggestion lookups and 60 checks an hour. Addresses the server has already looked up do not count.
- If a service asks your server to slow down, or fails three times in a row, the server stops contacting it for 15 minutes, doubling each time it happens again, up to 6 hours. A lookup that takes longer than 8 seconds is abandoned.
- komoot's public Photon service is free under fair use. A shop that needs more can run its own Photon server and enter its address under **Photon server**.

### After you update

- Nothing changes until an administrator switches address lookup on. It is off after the update, and no address leaves your server.
- Address lookup needs the shop server on this release. Your consoles update from your server as usual, and the server updates its own program when the shop is quiet. To update it now, open the Server Console on the server, go to **Settings** and click **Update everything on this PC** (under *Updates for this PC*). Until the server is updated, **Address lookup...** says that your shop server is older than this console, and the customer form shows plain address boxes.
- A console that has not updated yet shows no suggestions, even when the server has them switched on.

### Known issues

- **Tab moves into the list of suggestions, not to the next box.** With suggestions showing, pressing Tab in the Street box moves into the list, so an apartment or unit typed next is lost. Pressing Escape there - or after moving into the list with the arrow keys - closes the whole customer form without saving what you typed. Click in the Apartment or unit box instead of pressing Tab, and press Escape only while the cursor is in the Street box.
- **A quick Enter can bring the list back.** If you type and press Enter very quickly to take a suggestion, the list can reopen a moment later, and the next Enter takes its first suggestion - which may be the same street in another town. Check the City and ZIP before you click **Register** or **Save**.
- **The Address lookup... button can be cut off on smaller screens.** It is the last button on the Customers & fleet page. If you cannot see it, maximize the Server Console window.
- **A Photon server of your own must be version 1.1.0 or later.** An older one refuses every suggestion request, and the form then says the service asked the server to slow down. komoot's public Photon service is not affected.
- **A slow address service holds up that console.** While your shop server waits for a service to answer (up to 8 seconds), everything else that console asks the server waits behind it, so the Service Desk can pause for a few seconds while a street is typed. If your server cannot reach these services, switch address lookup off.
- Still open from earlier releases: unticking **Packages ship to this address** before **Mail goes to this address** can drop a typed shipping address (v0.440.0); after **Edit contact details** the Service Desk's customer card keeps showing the old details until the customer is opened again (v0.439.0); **Stop Safely** can be pushed off the screen in a console window narrower than about 780 pixels; **Choose machine...** on a ticket answers *No such machine.*; and an update can restart the console during a long Data Recovery job (v0.438.0).
