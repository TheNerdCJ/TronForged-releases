# v0.449.12 - TronForged 0.449.12 - Sales tax by state, Square, QuickBooks Online and safer updates

## TronForged 0.449.12 - Sales tax by state, Square, QuickBooks Online and safer updates

### Sales tax worked out by state, county and city

The United States has no federal sales tax: it is set by each state and by counties, cities
and special districts. TronForged can now work it out for every sale. Nothing changes until you
set it up - until then each invoice uses the single rate you type, exactly as before.

- **Set it up once** on the Server Console's new **Sales tax** page: your shop's address, the
  states you are registered in (with your permit number and how each state taxes a sale), and
  which kinds of items are taxable in each state. Parts are taxable by default. If a kind of item
  has not been answered for a state, an invoice that needs it is not sent until someone answers -
  TronForged never guesses a zero.
- **Rates come from free official sources**, and rules you enter always win:
  - the Streamlined Sales Tax rate and boundary files for its member states, applied on the
    dates they take effect;
  - the open TaxLocus sales-tax dataset ("Sales-tax data by TaxLocus - https://taxlocus.com"):
    a change is applied only when a person at your shop accepts it;
  - optional address lookups from the US Census Bureau and the Washington Department of Revenue,
    both off until you switch them on. Only the address where a sale is taxed is sent - never a
    name, email address or phone number, and never anything from a console.
- **Where the work happens** is chosen on each invoice: in store (the default, taxed at your
  shop), on site, shipped or remote, using the customer's address and the state's rule. An
  address typed on a site visit must be the street on one line and the city, state and ZIP code
  on the next; anything else in that box (a name or a note) is never sent to a tax lookup.
- **Tax is locked in when an invoice is sent.** A later rate change never alters an invoice you
  already sent; if you change its lines, the tax is worked out again at the rates it was sent
  with. Invoices sent before this update are never re-taxed.
- **Printed and emailed invoices** show one tax line per jurisdiction when more than one applies.
- **Exemption certificates.** Record a customer's resale or exemption certificate (with a copy of
  the form) in the customer screen. It shows when it is about to expire or has expired, and the
  invoice says "Tax exempt" with only the certificate's last four digits. Recording or revoking a
  certificate needs the tax.manage or accounting permission, the copy must be a PDF or a picture,
  and **View the certificate** opens the stored copy.
- **QuickBooks Online.** A new "sales tax by jurisdiction" export lists tax by state, county, city
  and district for your returns, and each state's tax can go to its own QuickBooks account. Sales
  tax is filed on the day it was locked in.
- **Square** receives each line's exact sales tax, and the daily Square check now warns you when
  Square's own counter charged a different rate from your shop's.
- **One cent fixed.** Some totals were rounded twice and came out one cent high (for example
  $13.00 at 7.65%). New invoices are rounded once; invoices already issued keep their amounts.

TronForged works the tax out from the rates and answers your server holds; it is not tax advice.
Check your first returns against your state's figures.

### Square is now optional, and does much more when you switch it on

Square stays off until you turn it on. On the Server Console's **Square** page there is a
**Use Square** switch and one switch for each job. With Square off (or a job switched off),
TronForged uses its own invoicing, payments, refunds and stock exactly as before.

If your shop already used Square for pay links or the Terminal, those keep working after this
update. Everything new below starts switched off.

- **Check connection** shows which Square account and location you are connected to, and
  whether it is Square's TEST system (sandbox) or PRODUCTION (shown in red). It also tells you if
  the access token is missing a permission a job needs.
- **Send invoices through Square.** "Send via Square" first shows a preview: every line, the tax,
  any discount and deposit, and Square's total next to TronForged's. It only sends when the two
  totals match to the cent. Square emails the invoice and takes the payment; the payment is
  recorded on the invoice automatically. You can ask for a deposit first. Voiding an invoice
  cancels it at Square too.
- **Refund through Square** from a Square card payment. A refund is recorded once, even if the
  connection drops part way.
- **Saved cards and maintenance plans.** Keep a customer's card on file (only from a Square
  payment they made in the last 24 hours, with their consent recorded) and put them on a monthly
  or yearly plan. Each plan charge becomes a TronForged invoice and payment.
- **Stock link.** Link stock items to Square's catalog; counts are kept in step, and sales rung
  up on Square's own counter come out of your TronForged stock.
- **Payouts and disputes.** See the money Square sent to your bank, with each payment's fee, and
  any chargebacks.
- **Square vs TronForged.** Every day the server compares Square's payments and refunds with
  TronForged's records and lists anything that does not match, so nothing goes missing. It never
  changes your records on its own. A payment another app took on your Square location is listed
  too, named by that app, so it is never mistaken for a counter sale.
- **Money items reach the right people.** A failed plan charge, a chargeback, a refund Square
  could not complete, or a difference found by the daily check now shows in the attention list
  for staff with billing or accounting rights.
- A pay link and a Terminal charge can no longer both be open on the same invoice.

Please try the new Square jobs on Square's sandbox first; the server guide has a step-by-step
walk-through.

### Estimates the customer approves

- **Save as estimate** in the invoice screen makes an estimate instead of an invoice. The
  customer can see it, and you can print it or email it (as an estimate, with how to approve it)
  and still change its lines. Nothing is owed on an estimate: it cannot be paid or sent through
  Square, and its parts stay in stock.
- The customer approves it in their own **My shop account** (My estimates), or you press
  **Record approval** and say how they agreed (in person, by phone or by email reply). It then
  becomes that same numbered invoice, once, even if two people approve it at the same moment.
  The invoice shows who approved it, how and when.
- If you send invoices through Square, the approved invoice goes through **Send via Square** the
  same way any invoice does, checked to the cent. Square never sees the estimate itself.
- An approval is for the total the customer was shown. If the estimate changed after they opened
  it, the approval is refused and they are asked to look at it again - nothing is approved and no
  deposit is taken.
- A deposit taken with an approval is checked like any other payment, and is taken only when the
  estimate can become the invoice.
- To withdraw an estimate, void it.

### A deposit that covers the parts

- The first money taken on a bill now covers its parts (the part lines, before tax); labour can
  be paid later. A bill with no parts needs no deposit.
- The invoice shows the parts deposit, Take payment asks for at least that, and Send via Square
  fills in the deposit with it. An approved estimate sent through Square asks for its parts first.
- Staff can take less on purpose with an override, which is recorded in the audit log.
- You can switch the rule off on the Server Console's Billing rules page (it is on by default). It
  works the same with Square on or off.

### Large refunds need a second person

- Once the refunds on one invoice add up to more than **$250.00** - recorded by hand or made
  through Square - that refund and every later one on the invoice waits until someone else who
  can manage users approves it. Splitting a large refund into smaller ones does not get around it. You can change the amount on the Server Console's new
  **Billing rules** page, which also lists the refunds waiting, with Approve and Decline.
- Nobody can approve their own refund. Both names are kept in the audit log.
- A Square refund that Square turned down and is tried again counts toward the limit like a new
  one, so it also waits for a second person when it takes the invoice over the limit.
- A refund can never be more than what was paid, less what has already been given back - refunds
  waiting for approval and Square refunds not yet confirmed included - even when two people
  refund the same payment at the same moment.
- A refund through Square is now recorded on the invoice when Square confirms it (usually within
  minutes), not before. A refund Square cannot complete is never recorded, and billing staff are
  told.

### QuickBooks Online: one monthly import, sales tax included

- A new **journal-entry export** for QuickBooks Online books every invoice, payment, refund and
  Square payout, with sales tax as its own liability. Card money waits in a "Square Clearing"
  account until Square's payout moves it to your bank, and Square's fees are booked as an
  expense, so your bank deposits match. Every payout must add up to the cent or it is marked
  "Needs review".
- A **sales tax summary** for any period: taxable and non-taxable sales, tax collected, tax
  refunded and net tax due.
- Account names follow QuickBooks Online's standard chart of accounts; you can change them on
  the Server Console. The accounting guide has a short "set up once, then import each month"
  section. Money Square holds for a dispute on one of your invoices goes to a "Chargebacks"
  expense account (create it once), not back onto what the customer owes.
- Switching Square off later does not change your books: Square payments and the payouts
  TronForged already has are still booked through Square Clearing to your bank, and stored
  counter sales stay in the sales tax summary.
- Sales rung up on Square's own counter are included: their sales tax goes into the sales tax
  summary on its own line, and tips are kept apart from sales. A Square payment TronForged
  cannot match to an invoice or a counter sale (for example one taken in another app) is
  marked "Needs review" instead of being counted as a sale with its tax in it; once its sale
  is imported, the next export moves it to the right accounts. A counter sale paid in cash
  (or another tender Square never pays out) is booked as a sale with its sales tax, not only
  as the cost of the part sold.
- **Exports are safe to open in Excel.** A customer name, item or note that begins with an
  equals, plus, minus or @ sign is written as plain text in every CSV export, so a spreadsheet
  can never run it as a formula. Amounts are unchanged.
- Every accounting export now uses your shop's own calendar day (an evening sale is no longer
  dated the next day) and includes every record in the period, however many there are.
- A refund takes back its share of the invoice's income and sales tax in the journal, so the
  sales tax in your books matches the sales tax summary after a part refund. A tip is never
  counted as a sale in the summary, and a draft invoice that is voided never changes it.
- You can now take a deposit at book-in or walk-in, before the bill is issued (**Take a deposit**).
  The QuickBooks Online journal books it to a **Customer Deposits** liability account (create it
  once in QuickBooks, or type your own name on the QuickBooks account names list) and applies it to
  the invoice, once, when the invoice is issued. Money taken after that is an ordinary payment.
- A deposit can also be taken through Square before the bill is issued - a pay link or a Terminal
  checkout for the parts deposit or any amount up to the bill - and when you record a customer's
  approval of an estimate (cash, card, check, transfer, or Square).
- Customers can have **payment terms**: Due on receipt (the default) or NET 15, 30, 45, 60 or 90.
  Each new invoice's due date is set from them, and the invoice's Payment due box starts on the
  customer's terms.
- Invoices and payments are marked as exported only after the Server Console has saved every
  export file. If a file cannot be saved, nothing is marked and your next export includes them
  again.

### Inventory that keeps your books right

- **Cost of goods for every sale.** Every part used on an invoice or sold over the counter now
  books its cost out of Inventory Asset in the QuickBooks Online journal, at the average cost of
  the day it left the shelf. A return puts it back. This works with Square on or off.
- **Shrinkage.** Stock corrections and cycle-count differences are booked to an "Inventory
  Shrinkage" account, with the reason as the memo.
- **Stock value report.** For any day: quantity on hand times average cost, per location, per
  category and in total, with a CSV export. Its total should equal Inventory Asset in QuickBooks
  Online - the accounting guide has the monthly check. If a part is used before it is
  received, the journal re-costs it when it arrives, so the two still match.
- **Categories.** Give stock items a category (for example Parts, Accessories, Refurbished
  devices). Each category can have its own QuickBooks income account and decides whether its
  parts are taxable by default. Linked items carry their category to Square.
- **Link to Square by SKU.** One screen lists the Square items whose SKU matches a TronForged
  item exactly, so you can link them all at once, and the Square items TronForged does not have,
  so you can bring them in. Imported items start at zero: count them in TronForged.
- **A count changed in Square's dashboard is no longer overwritten.** It is shown in the
  attention list, and that item stops syncing until you choose to keep TronForged's count or take
  Square's (which records a stock correction).
- **Change an unpaid invoice's lines.** Lines can be edited until a payment is recorded, a Square
  payment is open, or the invoice has been exported to QuickBooks. Stock follows the change.
- **An invoice and its stock are saved together.** If the stock cannot be saved, the invoice is
  not changed either and the console says so.
- **A voided invoice reaches QuickBooks Online.** Voiding an invoice you already exported adds a
  reversing entry to the next QuickBooks Online journal-entry export, and the sales tax summary
  shows the adjustment. The QuickBooks Desktop (.iif) and invoice CSV files have no reversing
  entry: if you import those, void the invoice in QuickBooks yourself.
- **Resend a purchase order** whose email failed.
- **A home location for each item**, so automatic reorders are delivered to the right store.

### Remote access after a restart, and the black screen

- After a PC restarts, the TronForged console can now take over remote access from the
  background service when someone signs in. Before, Windows refused it, so sessions could fail.
  This fix takes effect once the TronForged background service has restarted on the new
  version - at the PC's next restart at the latest. Until then the service that was already
  running keeps the old behaviour, and remote access may stay on the older background path for
  up to about 20 minutes after the console updates. Restarting the PC makes the change immediate.
- After a user signs out, the PC can be reached at the sign-in screen within seconds, not after two minutes.
- If the console is closed unexpectedly, the background service takes remote access back within
  seconds instead of waiting for the next restart.
- When someone signs in while a technician is still working at the sign-in screen, the console
  takes remote access over within about 30 seconds of that session ending, instead of up to 15 minutes later.
- The screen helper no longer starts somewhere it can only see a black screen.
- When remote access cannot start or the screen stays black, the console now says exactly why
  (for example, the .NET 10 runtime is missing, or the PC was never set up for unattended
  access), and the reason is kept in the PC's TronForged logs.
- Choosing which screen to watch on a PC with more than one monitor works again with the screen
  helper, and asking for the list of screens no longer cuts the helper's connection at the start of
  every remote session.
- When a technician connects just after a PC restarts and the screen helper is still starting, the
  session now waits for it (with a progress message every few seconds) instead of giving up after
  15 seconds.
- A large or busy screen that takes a moment to capture keeps its fast, partial-screen updates for the
  whole session instead of falling back to slower full-screen pictures, and a briefly stuck capture
  reconnects by itself.
- When someone signs in while the background service is still handing remote access over, the
  console keeps asking for up to 20 seconds instead of giving up after 3 and leaving remote access
  with the service for about two minutes.
- If the screen helper is stopped because the .NET 10 runtime is missing, the technician is told so,
  instead of being told it will be repaired automatically.
- A dark desktop whose only light is the taskbar is no longer mistaken for a black screen on large
  monitors.
- On a PC with more than one monitor, each new remote session now opens on the main screen, at
  full sharpness, instead of all screens shrunk into one window (or the screen the previous
  technician picked). The Screen menu still switches to any screen or to all of them. A technician
  console older than this version still opens on all screens.
- An idle remote session now uses almost no data: when the customer's screen is not changing, only
  a tiny "still connected" signal is sent every few seconds instead of the whole screen again
  (about 400 KB a second less on a four-monitor PC). A full picture is still refreshed once a minute.
  This now holds at the Windows sign-in and lock screens too.
- Very wide desktops (more than 8192 pixels across) and very busy screens no longer freeze the
  picture or drop the session; they fall back to whole-screen pictures automatically.
- The remote screen updates more smoothly while it is changing, uses less memory and processor time
  on the customer's PC, and is scaled more sharply in the remote desktop window.
- After a stuck capture, the next picture is a complete one, so no old patches of the screen are
  left behind.
- A screen helper that is slow to answer a screen list, a statistics request or Ctrl+Alt+Delete is
  asked again instead of being written off for the rest of the session.
- When a technician reconnects while the previous connection is still waiting for the screen
  helper, messages about the screen go to the new connection only.

### TronForged opens faster

- Opening TronForged while it is already running in the tray now shows it at once instead of
  after about 20 seconds.
- A normal start no longer re-checks every one of the program's files when nothing has changed
  since the last start. A file that is missing or was changed is still put back.
- The desktop and Start Menu icons are checked in the background, so the window is not kept
  waiting for them.
- Some housekeeping that used to run before the window appeared (tidying old work folders, and
  re-checking the permissions on screenshots, registry backups and private reports) now runs
  just after it opens. The connection secrets are still locked before anything reads them.
- The console's program code now comes ready-built, so less of it has to be prepared every time
  it opens. The download is about 4 MB larger.
- The Disk images and Forensics pages are now set up the first time you open them, not every time
  the console starts.
- A sleeping USB drive or an empty card reader no longer freezes the console while the Disk images
  page lists your drives and images.

### Safer updates

- An update can no longer leave a PC without its TronForged program. The old version is kept
  until the new one has fully started, and two failed starts roll back to it automatically.
- If a PC loses power or crashes in the middle of an update, Windows puts the previous version
  back when it restarts. An update also waits for your antivirus to finish checking the new
  version before it replaces the old one.
- If something does go wrong at start-up, the message now names the step that failed and the
  real error.
- Leftover files from older installs are moved aside so they cannot interfere.

### Adding a PC on a slow internet line

Adding a new PC now waits up to 25 seconds for your shop server and tries up to three times. If
your server answered but too slowly, it says the internet connection is very slow instead of
telling you to check the address. If nothing answered at all, it says that the connection may
be very slow, or that your server cannot be reached at that address (for example, a port that
is not forwarded on the shop's router, a server that is switched off, or a wrong public IP
address).

### Please note

None of the new Square features has been run against a real Square account yet, and the remote access fixes have not yet been tried on a real PC. Try them on
Square's sandbox before using them with real customers.

The QuickBooks Online journal-entry import has not yet been tried in a real QuickBooks Online
company. Import your first month into a test company and check it before
importing into your real books.

Sales tax has not yet been filed from TronForged's figures in a real state return. Set up one
state, compare a month's sales-tax-by-jurisdiction export with your state's figures, and only
then rely on it.
